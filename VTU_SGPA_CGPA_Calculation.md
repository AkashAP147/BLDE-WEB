# VTU SGPA & CGPA Calculation Guide (2018, 2021, 2022 Schemes)

This document explains how SGPA and CGPA are calculated for Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) under the 10-point grading system, applicable to the 2018, 2021, and 2022 schemes.

## Grading System
- VTU uses a 10-point grading scale:
  - A (Excellent): 10
  - B: 8
  - C: 7
  - D: 6
  - E: 5
  - F (Fail/Absent): 0

## SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)
- SGPA is calculated for each semester.
- For each subject:
  - **Credit Points** = Credits × Grade Points
- **SGPA Formula:**
  $$
  SGPA = \frac{\text{Sum of Credit Points for all subjects in the semester}}{\text{Total Credits in the semester}}
  $$

### Example
| Course Code | Credits | Grade | Grade Points | Credit Points |
|-------------|---------|-------|--------------|---------------|
| XX11        | 3       | B     | 8            | 3 × 8 = 24    |
| XX12        | 3       | F     | 0            | 3 × 0 = 0     |
| XX13        | 3       | A     | 9            | 3 × 9 = 27    |
| ...         | ...     | ...   | ...          | ...           |
| **Total**   | 20      |       |              | 101           |

$$
SGPA = \frac{101}{20} = 5.05
$$

## CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)
- CGPA is the weighted average of all SGPAs across semesters, weighted by the credits of each semester.
- **CGPA Formula:**
  $$
  CGPA = \frac{\text{Sum of (SGPA × Credits) for all semesters}}{\text{Total Credits across all semesters}}
  $$

### Example
| Semester | Credits | SGPA  | SGPA × Credits |
|----------|---------|-------|----------------|
| I        | 20      | 5.0   | 100            |
| II       | 20      | 6.73  | 135            |
| III      | 20      | 9.20  | 184            |
| ...      | ...     | ...   | ...            |
| **Total**| 120     |       | 1402           |

$$
CGPA = \frac{1402}{120} = 9.39
$$

## Special Note for Lateral Entry Students
- For lateral entry students (who join from the 3rd semester), the first two semesters are not considered in CGPA calculation.
